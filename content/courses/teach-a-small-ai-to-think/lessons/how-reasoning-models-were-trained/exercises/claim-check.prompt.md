# True or false: checking claims about R1

You will read a lot of confident posts about DeepSeek-R1. Many mix up details. Practise spotting them.

**What to do**

For each claim below, write **True** or **False**. For every false claim, write a one-sentence corrected version. Use the article and the R1 paper map cheat sheet; you do not need to read the full paper.

1. "R1 was trained with pure reinforcement learning, with no supervised fine-tuning at all."
2. "R1-Zero's rewards were checked by ordinary code, not by another AI."
3. "R1-Zero was given a reward for writing longer answers, which is why its answers grew."
4. "GRPO needs a separate critic model to estimate how good each answer is."
5. "R1-Zero's output was sometimes hard to read and mixed languages."
6. "The paper found that running RL directly on Qwen-32B beat distilling R1 into it."
7. "The language consistency reward slightly lowered benchmark scores, but the authors kept it."
8. "The authors reported that process reward models and Monte Carlo Tree Search worked better than outcome rewards."

**What "done" looks like**

Eight answers, each True or False, with a corrected sentence for every false one. Bonus: for one of the false claims, guess why people might believe it.
