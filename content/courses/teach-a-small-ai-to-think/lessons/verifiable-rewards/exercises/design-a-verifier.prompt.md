# Design a verifier for three tasks

Your capstone will train a reasoning model on a task you choose, and the first question will be: *can I grade it automatically?* Practise now.

**What to do**

For each task below, write:

1. **Verifiable?** Yes, partly or no.
2. **The check**, in plain words (no code needed): what the program compares, and what scores 1 vs 0.
3. **One leniency decision**: something you will accept even though it is not exactly the gold answer.
4. **One loophole**: a way a model could score well without really doing the task, and how you would close it.

The tasks:

- **A. Unit conversion.** "How many metres are in 3.2 km?"
- **B. Anagram solving.** "Rearrange the letters of LISTEN into another English word."
- **C. Writing a product description** for a pair of shoes in under 50 words.

**What "done" looks like**

A short table or three short paragraphs covering all four points for each task. For task C, say which parts (if any) you could still check automatically.
